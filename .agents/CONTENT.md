# CONTENT.md

## Purpose

Define the written language, storytelling, and content governance for the platform.

## Brand Voice

The voice should be:

- warm;
- human;
- hopeful;
- concrete;
- respectful;
- inclusive;
- transparent;
- optimistic without exaggeration.

It should not be:

- manipulative;
- guilt-driven;
- overly corporate;
- preachy;
- exaggerated;
- vague.

## Core Brand Message

> **SAMPAH KALIAN SANGAT BERARTI BAGI KAMI**

Use this as a signature statement, not as filler copy.

## Brand Story

The platform believes:

> Something that no longer has value to one person can become a resource for another.

The journey is:

```text
Yang tersisa
→ dikumpulkan
→ diolah
→ bernilai
→ dijual
→ menjadi manfaat
```

## Brand Architecture & Identity

```text
KITA TUMBUH (Master Brand)
└── KAMPUNG SMART FARMING (Platform / Ekosistem)
    └── Dari Limbah, Tumbuh Manfaat. (Tagline)
```

- **KITA TUMBUH**: Master brand gerakan sosial dan gotong royong sirkular.
- **KAMPUNG SMART FARMING**: Platform ekosistem pengolahan limbah, pertanian pintar komunitas, dan alokasi dampak.
- **Tagline**: *Dari Limbah, Tumbuh Manfaat.*
- **Core Emotional Statement**: *SAMPAH KALIAN SANGAT BERARTI BAGI KAMI*

## Community Story

Kampung Smart Farming di bawah naungan Kita Tumbuh mewakili keterhubungan antara rumah tangga, pemilahan limbah, dan kemandirian pangan warga.

Recommended narrative:

> **Dari yang tersisa, tumbuh sesuatu yang berarti.**

Supporting idea:

> **Mengolah potensi limbah, menumbuhkan kemandirian masyarakat.**

Smart farming adalah sarana pertanian presisi ramah lingkungan yang dikelola warga, bukan sekadar etalase teknologi.

## Copy Principles

### Be specific

Prefer:

> Minyak jelantah yang kamu donasikan akan masuk ke proses pengolahan sesuai kategori dan kondisi yang kami terima.

Avoid:

> Sampahmu akan menyelamatkan dunia.

### Explain the next step

Prefer:

> Pilih jenis limbah yang ingin kamu donasikan.

Avoid:

> Mulai perjalananmu sekarang.

### Show proof

Prefer:

> Donasi #DNT-2026-000124 telah diverifikasi pada 18 September 2026.

Avoid:

> Donasimu pasti memberikan dampak besar.

## Storytelling Structure

Use:

```text
Human situation
→ action
→ transformation
→ evidence
→ dignity
→ invitation
```

Do not use suffering as the main visual/copy mechanism.

## Beneficiary Language

Use dignified terms:

- penerima manfaat;
- peserta program;
- masyarakat;
- warga;
- individu/keluarga penerima dukungan.

Avoid language that reduces people to their hardship.

Avoid:

- "orang kasihan";
- "mereka yang malang";
- sensational descriptions.

## Impact Claims

Every impact claim must be traceable to a real record or approved calculation.

Do not write:

> "Setiap 1 liter minyak membantu 1 orang"

unless a verified, documented methodology supports the claim.

## Product Story

A product page should explain:

```text
WHAT
What is this product?

ORIGIN
What material/resource contributed to its creation?

VALUE
Why is it useful?

IMPACT
What social/environmental relationship is verified?

ACTION
Can the user buy/support/share?
```

## Transparency Copy

Explain financial terms accurately.

Distinguish:

- revenue;
- operating cost;
- production cost;
- social allocation;
- net result.

Never collapse them into one vague term such as "keuntungan" unless it is actually profit.

## CTA Vocabulary

Primary:

- Donasikan Limbah
- Mulai Berkontribusi
- Lihat Dampak

Secondary:

- Pelajari Cara Kerja
- Lihat Program
- Lihat Produk
- Lihat Transparansi

Avoid generic:

- Click Here
- Learn More everywhere
- Submit

Use action-specific language.

## Microcopy

### Empty

> Belum ada donasi. Saat kamu mulai berkontribusi, perjalanan donasimu akan muncul di sini.

### Success

> Donasi berhasil dibuat. Simpan ID donasimu untuk melihat perkembangannya.

### Error

> Donasi belum berhasil disimpan. Periksa koneksi atau coba lagi.

### Coming Soon

> Fitur ini sedang kami siapkan agar bisa digunakan dengan benar, bukan sekadar tersedia.

## SEO Content

Content should naturally answer real questions:

- limbah apa yang bisa didonasikan?
- bagaimana cara mendonasikan minyak jelantah?
- apa yang terjadi setelah limbah dikumpulkan?
- bagaimana hasil penjualan digunakan?
- apa itu Kita Tumbuh & Kampung Smart Farming?
- bagaimana smart farming mendukung komunitas kampung?
- bagaimana dampak dihitung secara transparan?

Do not create keyword-stuffed paragraphs.

## AI-Friendly Content

Important facts should appear in plain semantic text.

Prefer explicit statements:

> Kami menerima minyak jelantah, plastik tertentu, dan limbah organik sesuai ketentuan penerimaan.

Avoid vague brand-only statements.

## Editorial Governance

Content categories:

```text
brand copy
product copy
program copy
impact copy
operational instructions
legal/policy
SEO content
campaign copy
```

Legal/policy content should not be rewritten casually by a coding agent.

## Copy Review Checklist

```text
[ ] Is it understandable?
[ ] Is it factual?
[ ] Is it specific?
[ ] Is it dignified?
[ ] Is the CTA clear?
[ ] Does the claim have evidence?
[ ] Does it match the brand voice?
[ ] Does it avoid hype?
